One template, one type scale, and a design system page

- **Summary on the left everywhere:** posts now share the document template of the terms and the legal notice: the summary sits on the left of the text, follows the reading on wide screens, and folds above the text on phones.
- **One type scale:** nine text styles replace the 31 sizes written by hand across the site. One page title size everywhere (one step down for the long titles of posts), one size for every subtitle.
- **One page header:** the same trail, title, lead and meta line on every page; the portrait on the home page and the resume, as on the paper resume.
- **Fewer components:** one key / value sheet (profile, stack, identity, project sheet), one dated entry (changelog and lists of posts), one numbered section (posts and legal pages); the footer is on the home page too.
- **Admin › design system:** a new page, `/admin/design-system/`, shows the tokens and every component in day and night mode side by side. A test keeps it complete.
