Admin: leads first, created, edited and deleted from the list

- **Leads first:** the list of leads comes right under the key figures, with tabs that filter it by where each lead stands (to answer, to follow up, ongoing, closed). The weekly and per-need charts and the origins of the leads move to a "trend" section at the end.
- **Create a lead:** "+ Nouvelle demande" records a lead received by phone, by e-mail or on a platform, with its follow-up. It is forwarded to lotus like a lead sent through the site.
- **Edit a lead:** "Modifier" on each row opens its sheet: contact, need, language and message, then status, amount, follow-up date and notes. Wrong fields are named in a toast.
- **Delete a lead:** "Supprimer" on each row (or in the sheet), after a confirmation.
- **Audience on its own page:** `/admin/frequentation/` holds the audience figures, their breakdowns, the import buttons and the conversion funnel. The leads page keeps a 30-day recap (page views, visits, messages sent) that links to it.
