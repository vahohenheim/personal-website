Services page and offer block on the home page

Document template, summary on the left.
- **Home page:** a compact "offre" block after "ce que j’apporte": one sentence, three services with their starting price, and a link to the full page. The header has an `offre` link (`services` in English).
- **Prices written once:** every price lives in one file and is written in the typography of each language (`1 900 €`, `€1,900`); prices exclude VAT, which does not apply.
- **Structured data:** the page describes its offers as an `OfferCatalog` of services with their prices.
- **Components:** `FactGridComponent` (duration and prices of a service) and `OfferLineComponent` (one service on one line), both on the design system page.
