Custom websites and apps on the services page

- **New service:** "Création de site ou d’application sur mesure" ("Custom website or app"), fixed prices on a closed scope: landing page 1 200 €, showcase website (5 pages) 2 500 €, extra page 300 €, online shop from 5 500 €, web app from 9 000 €, maintenance 120 €/month. Shown on `/offre/` between headless development and production launch, and as a fourth line of the home block (from 1 200 €).
- **Starting prices:** a new `from` price (`dès 5 500 €`, `from €5,500`), described as a `minPrice` in the structured data. Options marked `addOn` (the extra page, the maintenance) never set the starting price of a service.
- **Texts:** the page and home leads mention the custom websites, in both languages.
- **Terms of service 1.1:** the creation of websites and apps and their monthly maintenance are now covered: new article 4.4 (subscription, notice, unused time, price revision, hosting on the provider's accounts and its transfer), feedback rounds in article 6, maintenance invoicing in article 9, article 12.1 and 17 aligned. New PDFs `cgp-1.1.pdf` and `cgp-1.1-en.pdf`; version 1.0 stays online.
